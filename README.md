**EMBERFALL** — a Lantern Warden arena survival game. Night falls, the Gloom pours in, and one lantern stands between the dark and dawn. Move with WASD or arrows, aim with the mouse — bolts fire on their own. Dash through danger with Space, Shift, or right-click (brief invulnerability). Banish the Gloom, gather its embers, level up and choose your light from a pool of 10 upgrades, and clear wave 10 to break the dawn — or keep fighting for score in endless mode. Wisps, husks, brutes, and elites escalate every wave; synthesized WebAudio sound and music, no audio files. Runs fully offline from static files — no network, no accounts, no downloads.

## Run it

```sh
npx serve game
# then open the printed localhost URL
# or: open game/index.html directly — everything is relative paths
```
